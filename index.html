Tools will keep changing. Judgment, character, creativity and the ability to work with others remain essential.

River Tech develops problem-solvers. Tink Town puts problem-solvers to work.

Our mission has two parts. River Tech is our school concept: preparing creative, capable graduates to put AI, automation and robotics to work, grounded in Christian faith and Idaho values. Tink Town is our business concept: bringing skilled people together to solve real problems for businesses and the community, from everyday improvements to ambitious moonshots.

Explore our two concepts below, or discover how you could help build them here in North Idaho.
