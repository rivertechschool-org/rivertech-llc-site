We offer a welcoming place to hang out, weekly gatherings where friendships grow and ideas take shape, and affordable purpose coaching to help you discover your gifts and put them to work.

Come for coffee and conversation. Bring the idea you can’t shake—or the questions you haven’t figured out yet. Find encouragement, discover your gifts and take practical steps toward the work God is calling you to do.
