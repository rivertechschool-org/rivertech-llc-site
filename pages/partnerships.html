Before you take the next step

Start with a
clear understanding.

Do all partnerships look the same?

No. A commercial project, a church workshop and an equipment lease serve different purposes. Each needs an arrangement that fits the work.

Can a partner work with students?

Activities involving a school require that school operator’s agreement, appropriate supervision and clear responsibilities. Possibilities include workshops, field trips, mentoring, practical projects and access to specialized equipment.

Can a business receive recognition or promote an offer?

That can be part of an agreed exchange. The participating school or organization approves the recognition, promotion and offers appropriate for its audience. A partnership does not create unrestricted access to students or families.

Could we contribute a building or equipment?

Yes. Our practical guide includes space plans, equipment capabilities and the arrangements we would need to discuss. See the seven opportunities ↗
