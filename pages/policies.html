1.1  Nondiscrimination Policy

Notice of Nondiscriminatory Policy as to Students

River Tech School admits students of any race, color, national and ethnic origin to all the rights, privileges, programs, and activities generally accorded or made available to students at the school, and does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national or ethnic origin in administration of its educational policies, admissions policies, scholarship and loan programs, and athletic and other school-administered programs.

River Tech School is a ministry of Faithful Five Inc., an Idaho nonprofit corporation.
