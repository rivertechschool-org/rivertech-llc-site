The River Tech approach

Human ability.
Technical fluency.

Emerging technology changes the tools. Education still has to develop the person who uses them.

River Tech combines creative expression, practical technology and rigorous learning within a Christian framework. The ambition is graduates prepared for responsibility, collaboration and work that matters.

Three pillars, held together

Develop the whole person.

Explore what each pillar contributes—and why none stands alone.

Human arts

A voice. A presence. A contribution.

Music and performance give students practice in preparation, expression, teamwork and showing up for others. These human skills belong beside technical ability.

In practice Rehearse, listen, refine and contribute to a shared performance.

Agentic technology

Build with the tools changing work.

Coding, robotics, design and AI belong in a practical learning experience. The aim is to understand a problem, choose a useful tool, check the result and take responsibility for the work.

In practice Design, build, test and explain the choices behind a project.

A strong foundation

Knowledge to think with.

Mathematics, language, science, history and Bible provide the knowledge students need to reason, communicate and make sound judgments. Technology is more useful when the person using it has something to bring.

In practice Work toward understanding, with room to practice and master the essentials.

What you receive

Permission to use the agreed River Tech name and branding, the school concept and consultation with Dan and Mary Hegelund on putting it into practice.

The concept brings Christian faith, academics, performing arts and technology together with preparation for adult life.

What you lead

Your organization operates the school. You choose staff, salaries, tuition, budgets and daily arrangements, and take responsibility for your premises and local requirements. River Tech LLC protects the brand and checks the agreed concept standards.

What we agree

A written licence identifies your approved campus, concept standards, fees and support. The licence is nonexclusive; additional campuses need written approval. We discuss the terms for your school before you commit.

From interest to opening

  1. Tell us your idea

    Share your community, experience and the school you want to build.

  2. Explore the fit

    Discuss the concept, your plans and what it would take to make the school work locally.

  3. Agree, then prepare

    Settle the licence and responsibilities before advertising or opening your River Tech school.

What would we be responsible for?

Your organization runs the school, including staffing, tuition, budgets, premises and local requirements. River Tech LLC provides the agreed brand and concept rights, founders’ consultation and oversight of the agreed concept standards.

What kind of space are we considering?

Our working plan starts with six classrooms averaging about 15 students each, plus space to eat, gather and work. The property guide includes a room-by-room plan and a preferred search range of 8,000–9,000 square feet. Explore the space plan ↗

Can a college start with a course rather than a school?

Yes. A workshop, course module or short course can be a starting point for collaboration. The college determines academic credit and qualifications. Explore college partnerships ↗
