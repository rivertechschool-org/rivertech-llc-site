Our story

A school was
the beginning.

Dan and Mary Hegelund’s work in education, music and community led to River Tech. Tink Town asks where that same belief in people can go next.

The founders

Dan & Mary
Hegelund

Educators, creators and builders with a shared Christian faith and a home in Post Falls, Idaho.

Their work brings together a conviction that education should develop the whole person and that people should have meaningful ways to put their abilities to use.

The thread through it all

People are capable of more when they have something worth learning, something worth making and a community to build with.

How the idea grew

From education
to enterprise.

2009

The original school proposal

While living in Sweden, Dan wrote a proposal for a Christian school bringing performing arts, technology and academics together.

2021

River Tech in practice

The River Tech program opened in Post Falls, Idaho, turning the educational idea into a school community.

2026

A home for the concepts

River Tech LLC provides a dedicated home for the school brand and concept, separate from individual school operators.

Introducing Tink Town

A business concept built around skilled people, useful technology and a welcoming place to work on real problems.

What we stand for

Christian faith. Idaho roots.

Our work is grounded in Christian faith and the Idaho values of family, church, community, hard work, patriotism and personal responsibility. We believe in merit, grit and entrepreneurship: developing our gifts, meeting difficult challenges and building things that serve others. We value truth, human dignity and honest discussion. Technology should strengthen real life, never replace family, friendship, faith or time outdoors. These convictions shape how we teach, how we do business and the partnerships we choose.
