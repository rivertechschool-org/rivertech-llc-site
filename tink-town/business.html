GOOD QUESTIONS.

LET’S MAKE
IT CLEAR.

Do we need to replace our software?+

Not necessarily. We begin with what you already use. Any new tools, subscriptions and ongoing costs belong in the proposal.

How do we know an improvement works?+

Agree what success means, try a small pilot, and compare the time, effort, quality and review required before and after.

What about privacy and human judgment?+

We agree the information a tool may use and where a person must review the result. Please remove private customer or staff information from examples you send for an initial conversation.

Can our team learn to do this?+

Yes. Practical training and handover can be part of the project. We agree the learning goals and support you need.

What does it cost?+

That depends on the task, the systems involved and the support required. We define a useful first result and agree a price before starting.
