THREE WAYS TO BEGIN.

What would help
you move forward?

01

A place to work & belong

We are building toward a welcoming third place: coffee, shared tables, useful tools and regular gatherings. Somewhere to discover what others are making and find people to build with.

A POSSIBLE START

A coffee and a conversation become an introduction to someone who knows how to help with your idea.

Explore the possibilities +
  • Coffee and conversation — A welcoming place to spend time and discover what others are making.
  • Shared workspace — Tables and workspaces where ideas and collaborations can develop.
  • Weekly gatherings — Build friendships and give ideas room to take shape.
02

Events that bring the future closer

Host a team or client gathering, create a workshop, or explore an Agentic Mindset evening. Combine good company with demonstrations, practical challenges and ideas people can use.

A POSSIBLE START

A company brings its team for a meal, a useful introduction to AI and an exercise based on a task they handle at work.

Explore the possibilities +
  • Company events — Host staff or clients in a setting built around useful ideas and technology.
  • Public events and workshops — Create gatherings for the wider community.
  • Agentic Mindset evenings — Dinner, a keynote, live demonstrations and a guided group exercise.
  • Recurring team sessions — Return monthly or quarterly to explore changes and review practical experiments.
  • Performances and talks — Use a small stage for creative and community gatherings.
03

Faith & community in action

Make room for prayer, devotionals, Christian talks and worship, alongside practical learning and service. Connect with churches, farmers, craftspeople and outdoor groups around work that helps the community.

A POSSIBLE START

A church group combines a technology workshop with a devotional, or a local group shares its knowledge through a hands-on service project.

Explore the possibilities +
  • Worship support — Draw on the River Tech performing-arts connection, with delivery agreed.
  • Prayer and devotionals — Include Christian talks, prayer and devotionals in an agreed gathering.
  • Faith and technology events — Combine practical learning with Christian reflection and worship.
  • Outdoor and agricultural projects — Work with farmers, hunters, hikers, craftspeople and outdoor groups.
  • Community service and demonstrations — Share knowledge and make things that help people care for the local area.
