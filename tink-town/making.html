01

Design & prototype

Work from a sketch, file, sample or conversation. We can help develop the model, choose a sensible first version and make a prototype you can test.

A POSSIBLE START

A broken mounting bracket becomes a measured model and a test part, ready to check for fit.

Explore the possibilities +
  • Design from a sketch or idea — Work out dimensions, materials and what the object needs to do.
  • 3D modeling — Prepare a digital model for a physical object.
  • Prototypes and test models — Make an early version to try and refine.
02

Make, build & tinker

Custom parts, useful objects, robotics projects and supplies for your own ideas. The process might use 3D printing, cutting, machining or a specialist production partner.

A POSSIBLE START

A custom enclosure for a small electronics project, with the openings, mounting points and finish it needs.

Explore the possibilities +
  • Custom and replacement parts — Assess a broken part or a specific need and possible production methods.
  • Brackets, fixtures and enclosures — Make practical parts for holding, protecting or supporting things.
  • Panels, signs and displays — Produce useful objects and display pieces.
  • Filament 3D printing — Build suitable objects layer by layer from filament.
  • Resin 3D printing — Produce suitable detailed parts, with washing and curing.
  • Fiber laser cutting — Cut suitable metal parts, brackets, panels and signs.
  • CO₂ laser cutting — Cut appropriate materials such as wood and acrylic.
  • Waterjet cutting — Cut suitable materials using an abrasive waterjet.
  • CNC routing — Shape wood, plastics and composite panels.
  • CNC milling — Machine three-dimensional parts from solid stock.
  • Sheet-metal bending — Form sheet parts with a press brake.
  • Parts finishing and assembly — Deburr, tap, countersink, insert hardware and assemble.
  • Robotics and electronics projects — Bring tools and appropriate specialist expertise to practical projects.
  • Maker supplies — Sell robotics components, modeling parts and useful supplies.
03

Print & signs

Flyers, menus, business cards, posters, banners and signs—designed and produced around their job, with finishing and larger-format work arranged as needed.

A POSSIBLE START

One event, with the right mix of small handouts, a clear entrance sign and posters people can actually read.

Explore the possibilities +
  • Everyday business printing — Flyers, brochures, business cards, menus and booklets.
  • Large-format printing — Posters, banners, signs and displays.
  • Vinyl lettering and decals — Produce adhesive graphics for suitable applications.
  • Print finishing — Trim, fold, crease, bind, laminate and mount.

Our production capability is being developed in stages. Compact printing and selected 3D work can come before a full fabrication shop. Larger or specialist orders may be produced through an outside specialist.

BEFORE WE BEGIN.

BRING WHAT
YOU HAVE.

Do I need a finished design file?+

No. A drawing, photo, sample or explanation may be enough to begin a design conversation. We will identify what still needs to be measured or designed.

Can you repair anything?+

Bring us the problem and we can assess it. A replacement part or a new approach may help, but Tink Town is not offering a blanket appliance or electronics repair service.

Do you own all the machines listed?+

No. The wider equipment range is a direction for growth. Each project needs a confirmed production route and the relevant expertise before we commit.
