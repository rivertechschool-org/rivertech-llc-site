BEFORE WE BEGIN.

ROOM TO
BEGIN.

Do I need a business idea?+

No. You might bring a ministry idea, a community need, a creative project or a question about how to use your gifts.

Can Christian faith be part of the conversation?+

Yes. Faith is part of our foundation, and we welcome conversations about calling, stewardship and serving others. You are also welcome to bring a practical idea without having all the spiritual language figured out.

What would a first conversation cover?+

Where you are now, what matters to you, and what a useful next step might be. The coaching format, price and follow-up are agreed before beginning.
