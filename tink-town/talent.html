BEFORE WE BEGIN.

REAL PEOPLE.
CLEAR TERMS.

Is this a job board with current vacancies?+

No. We are developing the talent network and placement approach. An enquiry begins a conversation; it does not mean a particular vacancy is available.

Who makes the hiring decision?+

The employer. Introductions require the candidate’s permission, and the employer interviews and makes its own decision.

Would candidates pay a placement fee?+

The proposed model is employer-paid, with any fee and terms agreed before an introduction. Candidates would not pay a placement fee.

Are jobs guaranteed to students or graduates?+

No. Readiness, opportunities and the requirements of the work all matter. School involvement also needs the operator’s agreement and suitable supervision.
